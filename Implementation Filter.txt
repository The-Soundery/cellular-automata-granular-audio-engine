These are NOT harness failures. Automated gates are green. Do not "fix" them
by fitting thresholds to saturated-δ test patterns.

1. Chaos under-spend on real Utomata (δ ≈ 0.1)  — SUPERSEDED, see header.
   The δ ≈ 0.1 premise is false. Measured live: chaos pool δ̄ 0.26–0.30,
   t 0.75–0.87, under-spend ~1 dB not ~5 dB. Do not lower deltaRateNorm on
   the strength of this entry. Retained verbatim below only so the correction
   is traceable.
   chaosPackRateHz multiplies by t = clamp01(meanDelta / 0.35).
   Test patterns saturate δ so t = 1 and Gate 3 looks balanced. On real sims at
   δ ≈ 0.1, t ≈ 0.29 → chaos spends under a third of its area share (~5 dB down).
   Symptom: live CA chaos feels thin vs calm/texture despite area share meters.
   Lever: SCHED.deltaRateNorm (0.35) and/or the t term in chaosPackRateHz.
   Also serves as a bag crossfade — do not delete blindly. Measure on real
   Utomata, not full-flicker / half-half.

2. Frozen-and-detailed vs still-and-uniform character (complaint 3)
   Headless: frozen-noise mean Q 1.32 < uniform-static 8.00 (PASS).
   Open: whether they *read* as different materials to the ear at matched
   loudness. Sim: frozen-noise vs uniform-static. If they sound the same,
   spectralT→Q / material law needs listening, not a louder frozen field (D1).

3. Percussive chaos + blinker envelope

Proposed Brief addendum (pending owner listen / adopt)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Neutrality is measured, not assumed. Any mapping axis that changes output
  energy is a bug until proven flat. The engine compensates filter bandwidth;
  it does not compensate the source spectrum.
- Area share is spent, not merely allocated. Caps that prevent a pool from
  reaching its share are loudness policy in disguise.
- Two axes, two consequences: timeOrder → duration/envelope; similarity → Q.
  That is what makes still-and-uniform ≠ still-and-detailed at matched level.
- Sampling must not manufacture change. Stratified sites are re-derived every
  frame from the measured area — not listening posts, never owned by a grain.
