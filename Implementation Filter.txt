------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Executive verdict
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The current pipeline implements Creative Brief V2 (structures → tracked IDs →
scored voice allocation → ~32 identity-owned grains). V3 rejects that ontology.

V3 requires a renderer / information-compression model:
CA field → finite equal-share grain lattice → preserve local/relational
information → emergent psychoacoustic structures.

Luminance is NOT volume. Do not gate or weight amplitude by brightness.

Largest required shift: replace StructureTracker + StructureAllocator “composer”
logic with a neutral field renderer, and harden the AudioWorklet against pops/
clicks under high grain counts and varying simulations.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Target architecture (V3)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Utomata (visual CA)
      ↓
  FrameObserver (field snapshot)
      ↓
  FieldReducer / GrainPlan builder  ← NEW: compression, not voice allocation
      ↓
  AudioEngine (main thread bridge)
      ↓
  grain-processor (robust finite-grain renderer)
      ↓
  speakers

Structures may emerge in the ear. They must not be selected, scored, or owned
by the engine.

Concrete REMOVE list (V2 composer machinery)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Delete or stop calling (future pass):

- Structure identity matching, coasting, tip/axis ownership
- presence / calmBonus / infoWeight scoring
- VOICE_BUDGET_ORDERED and fieldOrder-scaled budgets
- masterGain softened by fieldOrder
- grain length / overlap / persistence / motion shaped by global order
- stable vs chaotic allocation pools
- “structure deserves probes” hysteresis as curation
- Interestingness-driven deferral that prefers some loci over others for taste

Allowed replacements must answer only:
“How do we fit the field into N grains and energy E without inventing taste?”

Grain length / overlap (Brief V3 addition):
- Continuity must come from field presence/self-similarity over time; activity
  from field change — not from stability→pad or chaos→glitch scoring.
- Allowed: uniform physically constrained aperture, and/or local refresh/aperture
  from measured local temporal change.
- Worklet + reducer must update with the field so coherent regions can fuse and
  changing regions can redistribute; owned frozen voices are a V3 failure.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REWRITE sketch: FieldReducer (replacement for tracker+allocator)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Inputs: current (and maybe previous) RGB field, grid size, grain budget N,
        energy budget E, CPU budget signal if available.

Outputs: GrainPlan = list of up to N grains, each with:
  - x, y (grid locus)
  - amplitude share from local / relational energy (sum-normalised to E)
  - r, g, b for micro-material only
  - no structureId ownership; no stability score; no interestingness rank

v3.0 compression (locked):

Uniform lattice over the full grid; always emit all lattice points; equal
amplitude share 1/N; RGB = micro-material only; localDelta → refresh only.

Later (not v3.0): hierarchical collapse of near-identical neighbours if needed.

Success test: a river occupying ~40% of grid cells should contain ~40% of
lattice grains (spatial occupation), each still at equal 1/N — never labeled
a structure, never louder because brighter.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Reliability / glitch work (practical sacred)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Independent of philosophy, V3 requires clean audio:

- Audit grain starts/stops, buffer swaps, plan replacement, and wrap crossfades
- Ensure plan updates cannot click (equal-power or zero-crossing strategies)
- Cap work per audio block hard (CPU budget as a real limiter, not a wish)
- Stress test: empty field, full field, rapid chaos, long stable bands, reset,
  source reload, enable/disable audio
- Prefer fewer fragile special cases over stacked continuity EMAs that hide bugs
