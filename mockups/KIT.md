# Mockup kit — archive of the locked sketch

Live app is `src/`. These HTML stills were the visual lock before the HUD
was built. Do not treat them as the running instrument.

Chosen spec: `mockups/chosen/main.html` and `mockups/chosen/explore.html`.
A/B/C were discarded comparison drafts.

## Visual law (still binding)

- Colour lives **only** in the CA. Chrome, type, icons, background are monochrome.
- Background: dark grey `#1c1c1c`. Ink `#cfcfcf`, mute `#7a7a7a`, line `#3a3a3a`.
- No box-shadow, glow, blur, gradient, rounded-xl, glass, gold chrome.
- Overlay colours only when overlay is on: calm `#c4a35a`, static `#96969b`,
  chaos `#e64824`, osc `#3782be`, flow `#46af78`. Outlines, not fills.
- Chrome type: VT323. Equation + coord: Share Tech Mono.

## What shipped vs the sketch

- No separate Focus mode. Main is the instrument; Explore is the telescope.
- Overlay off by default. Waveform + grains sit under the field, not on it.
- DATA fold: regime %, spend, budget, clip, step, SIM. Not δ̄ / κ / sat.
- Neighbor click travels; centre click settles. Audio follows centre only.
