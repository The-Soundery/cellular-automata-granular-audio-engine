Cellular Automata Granular Audio Engine — Creative Brief (V3)
Status: AUTHORITATIVE north star. Supersedes Creative Brief.txt (V1) and Creative Brief v2.txt (V2).

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Project Overview
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This project is a browser-based audiovisual instrument that lets a listener hear
a Cellular Automata simulation as a finite sonic universe.

Core axiom (unchanged):

  The audio must NOT be controlled by the Cellular Automata.
  The audio IS the Cellular Automata.

Visual output and audio output are two sensory renderings of the same underlying
mathematical system. Granular synthesis is the chosen listening medium — not a
synthesizer that the CA modulates.

What you are listening to:

  The redistribution of sonic energy throughout the Cellular Automata universe.

Computational structures are not curated musical objects. They emerge when the
simulation produces coherent spatial organisation, and they become audible when
neutral information reduction preserves that organisation in a finite grain field.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Core Design Philosophy
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We are NOT building:

- A granular synthesizer intelligently playing the Cellular Automata
- An audiovisual music visualiser
- A composer, curator, or interestingness engine
- Explicit structure detection that asks which structure deserves a voice
- Aesthetic preference for wash, stability, chaos, beauty, or intensity
- Arbitrary DSP mappings disconnected from CA mathematics
- Voice ownership, structure prioritisation, interestingness scores,
  stable-vs-chaotic allocation pools, or sustain-vs-active voice systems

We ARE building:

- One Cellular Automata universe
- One shared mathematical state
- Two renderers: Visual Renderer and Audio Renderer
- A physically constrained audio observer that compresses the simulation into
  sound the way a renderer / image compressor reduces an image into a finite
  display — preserving as much local and relational information as possible


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Audio Engine as Constrained Observer
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The audio engine does not decide what deserves to be heard.

It asks only:

  How much of the simulation can be faithfully rendered into sound
  at any given moment?

Its only legitimate constraints are physical and perceptual:

- Maximum grain count
- Maximum total sonic energy
- Maximum CPU budget
- Human perceptual limitations

Reduction must use simple, mathematically explainable rules — not artistic
judgement about what is musical, ambient, beautiful, chaotic, or interesting.

Similar information may naturally collapse together. Large coherent regions may
naturally dominate the sonic field. That emergence is a consequence of faithful
compression, not a decision that a structure was “worth” a voice.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Spatial Topology (Sacred Mapping)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Listening loci are positions in the CA’s spatial field.

X axis — Sample position
  Left side of the simulation = beginning of the source audio.
  Right side of the simulation = end of the source audio.
  Horizontal movement becomes movement through the source material.

Y axis — Frequency / spectral position
  Bottom of the simulation = lower frequencies.
  Top of the simulation = higher frequencies.
  Vertical movement becomes spectral movement.

These mappings are sacred. Topology in the simulation must remain audible as
topology in the sound. The listener should be able to follow motion through the
finite sonic universe without hearing curated “chosen” objects.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Colour (Sacred Fence)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Colour describes what a locus is made of, not how it behaves.

Colour MAY influence only micro-temporal material properties of individual grains:
- Grain envelope shape
- Internal energy distribution within the grain
- Subtle sonic texture / “colour of the sound”

Colour must NEVER influence:
- Frequency / spectral position
- Sample position
- Grain length
- Macro-temporal behaviour (retriggering, persistence, energy flow)
- Which loci are rendered (allocation / prioritisation)
- Global / master energy
- Amplitude / loudness (including luminance-as-volume)

Luminance and brightness are not volume. They are part of colour/material only.
The engine must not silence dark cells or boost bright cells. Every listening
locus in the finite grain lattice contributes continuously with equal sonic
share; large coherent regions become present because many neighbouring grains
agree in space and material — not because they are louder per cell.

Material differences should be perceptible as texture, without sounding like
decorative DSP effects or a parallel timbral engine.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Stability, Chaos, and Neutrality
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The engine must not impose a sonic aesthetic on the simulation.

Stable patterns must not be turned into ambient pads because they are stable.
Chaotic patterns must not be turned into glitch music because they are chaotic.

Stability and chaos only redistribute sonic energy differently:

- Patterns that remain present continue to occupy sonic space because they
  remain present in the simulation.
- Patterns that change rapidly redistribute their sonic energy more rapidly
  because the underlying state is changing.

Neither regime is more important or more musical. If a computationally dominant
stable pattern makes the output more continuous, that continuity is an inevitable
consequence of the simulation — not an engine preference. If the simulation is
more active, the sound should be more active for the same reason.

The simulation determines the musical outcome. The engine reveals it.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Grain Length, Overlap, and Temporal Continuity
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This must be stated explicitly, because it will not automatically happen from
topology and colour alone.

Granular synthesis always has a temporal aperture (grain length) and a density
of successive grains (overlap / refresh). These are properties of the listening
medium — analogous to pixel size and frame integration in an image renderer —
not aesthetic preferences for ambient or glitch music.

Required emergence (must actually occur in implementation):

1. Coherent, slowly changing regions must be able to form blended, continuous
   sonic mass. This happens because the same (or highly similar) local field
   state keeps being rendered at nearby loci over time, so successive grains
   reinforce one another psychoacoustically.

2. Rapidly changing regions must sound more active and fragmented. This happens
   because the local field state at those loci is changing, so successive grains
   carry different information and sonic energy redistributes more quickly.

The cause of continuity is presence and self-similarity of the simulation over
time. The cause of activity is change in the simulation over time.
Neither outcome may be imposed by scoring regions as “stable = pad” or
“chaotic = glitch.”

Allowed ways to realise this (neutral, mathematically explainable):

- A fixed or globally constrained grain aperture and overlap set by physical
  limits (CPU, grain budget, perceptual fusion), applied uniformly — with the
  field’s own persistence/change producing continuity vs activity; and/or
- Local grain refresh / temporal aperture derived from local temporal change
  already present in the CA field (how quickly this locus is evolving), so that
  slowly changing loci update their sonic contribution more slowly and rapidly
  changing loci update more rapidly — as measurement of change, not as taste.

Forbidden:

- Lengthening grains, raising overlap, or holding voices because a stability
  score, fieldOrder, or “musical sustain” preference says so
- Shortening grains or forcing chatter because a chaos/interestingness score
  says so
- Letting colour control grain length or macro refresh (colour remains
  micro-material only)
- Freezing sonic content at a locus after the simulation there has moved on,
  or ignoring field updates so the ear hears owned voices instead of the field

Success test for this section:

If a river-like form remains present and drifts slowly, the listener hears a
coherent moving sonic mass without a sustain engine being told to hold it.
If the same region dissolves into rapid local change, that sonic mass
redistributes and fragments because the underlying information changed —
not because an allocator switched it into a “chaotic pool.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Information Reduction Model
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Conceptual pipeline:

  Cellular Automata grid
      ↓
  Reduce to a finite grain budget
      ↓
  Preserve as much local and relational information as possible
      ↓
  Allow psychoacoustic grouping and grain overlap to form emergent sonic structures

Example scale (illustrative, not a fixed requirement):
a large CA field reduced into hundreds of grains (order of 500–1000), within CPU
and energy limits.

Expected emergent consequences (not targets to optimise for):

Large coherent regions → larger sonic textures, more continuous spectral masses,
more perceptual presence — because they occupy more of the field.

Small chaotic regions → fine detail, rapidly evolving textures, localised events —
because they occupy less space and change more often.

Nothing is prioritised because it is interesting, stable, or musical.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Global Energy
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The sonic universe contains a finite amount of energy.

Master / global energy handling exists only as a physical ceiling / normalisation
for the whole render (no clipping, practical loudness). It is not a per-cell
volume system and must never use luminance, interestingness, or stability to
decide who is loud.

Equal-share lattice grains sum into that finite ceiling. Spatial occupation —
how much of the field a region covers — creates sonic presence. Brightness does
not.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source Audio
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The user may load WAV, MP3, or AIFF.

Source audio is the raw material redistributed across the finite sonic universe
by the CA’s spatial topology (X → sample, Y → spectrum) and coloured locally by
grain material (RGB → micro-temporal texture only).

No traditional “effects rack” is required. Processing exists only as part of
faithful rendering (e.g. granular windowing, spectral locus selection by Y).


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Visual Engine
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Utomata remains the Cellular Automata visual engine.

It provides cell positions, RGB values, neighbour relationships, temporal
evolution, global state, editable equations, and toroidal topology.

The audio renderer observes the same state. It does not drive the CA.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
User Interaction (V3 surface)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Users should be able to:

- Move through CA equation variations
- Restart / reset the simulation
- Load source audio
- Observe audiovisual evolution in real time

The creative instrument is the simulation. The audio engine is the listener’s
constrained ear, not a second performance layer of aesthetic knobs.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sacred / Negotiable / Forbidden
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SACRED
- Audio IS the CA (two renderers, one state)
- Listen to redistribution of sonic energy in a finite sonic universe
- Neutral information reduction (renderer / compressor metaphor)
- X → sample position, Y → spectral position
- Colour → micro-temporal grain material only
- Luminance is not volume; continuous equal-share field listening
- No aesthetic imposition (beauty / wash / chaos / interestingness)
- Reduction constrained only by grain count, sonic energy, CPU, perception
- Continuous sonic universe (not disconnected curated snapshots)
- Continuity from field presence/self-similarity; activity from field change
- Robust, glitch-free audio under varying simulations (practical sacred)

NEGOTIABLE
- Exact grain budget and grid resolution (physical/CPU limits)
- Exact compression algorithm that preserves local/relational information
  (uniform sampling, energy-faithful sampling, hierarchical collapse, etc.)
  provided it introduces no interestingness / stability / musical scoring
- Whether grain aperture is globally fixed by physical limits, or locally
  coupled to measured local temporal change (both allowed; taste-scoring not)
- Subtlety range of colour→envelope material (must remain audible as texture,
  not spectral processing)
- Toroidal continuity implementation details
- UI metering / debug overlays (must not redefine the listening philosophy)

FORBIDDEN
- Structure detection that selects which structures deserve voices
- Voice ownership / identity systems whose purpose is curation
- Interestingness, presence, calm, or chaos scores that change priority
- fieldOrder-like global aesthetic priors (settle → fewer/calmer/prettier voices)
- Stable-vs-chaotic or sustain-vs-active allocation pools
- Using grain length/overlap as an aesthetic pad/glitch designer
- Colour affecting frequency, sample position, grain length, macro-timing,
  allocation, amplitude, or global energy
- Luminance → volume, brightness silence gates, or “lit cells only” listening
- Using the CA as a modulation source for unrelated musical processes
- Optimising for ambient beauty, glitch excitement, or “musicality”


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Success Checklist (listening)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The instrument is working when:

1. A large coherent form drifting diagonally is hearable as motion through
   sample space (X) and spectrum (Y) without feeling “selected.”
2. Its material subtly changes as its colours change — as texture, not as
   filter/allocation behaviour.
3. Smaller regions contribute detail in proportion to their presence.
4. The overall sound reflects the overall state of the simulation at that moment.
5. Continuity feels like the simulation continuing, not like a voice engine
   holding notes because stability was scored highly.
6. Chaos sounds more active only when the simulation is more active.
7. The listener thinks: “I am hearing the simulation move,” not
   “the algorithm chose this.”
8. Audio remains clean: no pops, clicks, or instability across regimes.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Failure Checklist (listening)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The instrument is failing when:

1. It sounds like a granular synthesizer intelligently performing the CA.
2. Output feels like disconnected snapshots rather than one sonic universe.
3. The ear can hear curation: favourites, sustained pads-for-stability,
   glitch-for-chaos, or interestingness.
4. Colour behaves like spectral DSP, allocation control, or macro timing.
5. Topology motion is not followable, or feels quantized into chosen probes.
6. The mix does not correspond to what dominates the visual field.
7. Pops, clicks, dropouts, or brittle behaviour under different equations.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Final Creative Goal
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Create an audiovisual instrument that behaves as a miniature computational
universe with a finite sonic energy budget.

The beauty of the visuals emerges from simple local relationships between cells.
The beauty of the sound emerges from those same relationships, heard through
neutral information reduction — not through composition.

Version 3 should feel like listening to the Cellular Automata itself:
the movement and evolution of computational organisation through a finite
sonic universe, rendered as faithfully as physics and perception allow.
