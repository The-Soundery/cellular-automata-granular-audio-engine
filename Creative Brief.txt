Cellular Automata Granular Audio Engine — Creative Brief
Status: AUTHORITATIVE. Sonic Laws below are the current truth for audio behaviour.

Identity:

  HSV → polar material plane (hue angle · sat radius · value band)
  saturation → window half-width · regime soft-bias (calm→sustained, chaos→transient)
  X → stereo pan + source L/R channel mix (both follow region-bound grains)
  Y → absolute log bandpass (80 Hz bottom … 12 kHz top); independent of material centroid
  sample window frozen at spawn

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Project Overview
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This project is a browser-based audiovisual instrument that lets a listener hear
a Cellular Automata simulation as a finite sonic universe.

Core axiom:

  The audio must NOT be controlled by the Cellular Automata.
  The audio IS the Cellular Automata.

Visual output and audio output are two sensory renderings of the same underlying
mathematical system. Granular synthesis is the chosen listening medium — not a
synthesizer that the CA modulates.

What you are listening to:

  The movement and evolution of computational information through time —
  emergence, propagation, interaction, and transformation — heard as sonic
  events / grains that emerge from that activity.

Cellular Automata are not interesting because of the static state of individual
cells. They are interesting because of continuous computational flow. The sound
must evolve as the visuals evolve.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Core Design Philosophy
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We are NOT building:

- A granular synthesizer intelligently playing the Cellular Automata
- An audiovisual music visualiser
- A composer, curator, or interestingness engine
- Explicit structure detection that asks which structure deserves a voice
- Aesthetic preference for wash, stability, chaos, beauty, or intensity
- Arbitrary DSP mappings disconnected from CA mathematics
- Voice ownership, structure prioritisation, interestingness scores,
  stable-vs-chaotic loudness pools, or sustain-vs-active voice systems
- Fixed listening points, persistent sonic locations, or lattice “ears”
- Luminance → volume as a substitute for hearing computational flow

We ARE building:

- One Cellular Automata universe
- One shared mathematical state
- Two renderings: visual and audio
- A physically constrained audio renderer of computational evolution
- An instrument whose sonic complexity emerges from CA behaviour itself
- Observational regions (coherence / chaos / texture / oscillators / flow)
  as field measurements that set grain laws — never as owned voices


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sonic Laws (authoritative)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

One-sentence axiom:

  The CA field is observed as coherence regions and chaotic areas; each area’s
  share of a finite grain pool is proportional to its size; within that share,
  change and coherence set rate, length, and overlap; grain identity at spawn
  is HSV→polar material, X→pan+channel-mix, Y→absolute spectrum; sample window
  freezes at spawn while region-bound grains directly follow pan/Y/channel-mix
  with their region; loudness is globally neutral.


1. Grains are ephemeral carriers of identity

   A grain is a short-lived sonic event. When it spawns it carries identity from
   the field at that moment. It does not own a place in the grid. The next grain
   may carry different identity. There are no persistent sonic posts.


2. Grain identity (what the grain is made of)

   From the spawn cell (or calm spawn site):

   - HSV → sample position / locked scrub window via a polar descriptor plane
           (hue = angle on a centroid-ranked circle; saturation = radius from
           centre so grey still reaches material via value; value = energy
           band). Soft regime bias prefers sustained material for calm/texture
           and transient material for chaos/osc — FROZEN at spawn
   - Saturation → window half-width in seconds (grey = diffuse / wide;
                  saturated = focused / tight) — FROZEN at spawn
   - X        → stereo pan (−1 left … +1 right) AND source L/R channel mix
                (left of grid hears left of recording). Initial at spawn;
                while a grain belongs to a region it directly follows that
                region’s motion in pan and channel mix (spawn offset preserved)
   - Y        → bandpass cutoff as an absolute log sweep of the frequency
                spectrum (80 Hz at the bottom of the grid, 12 kHz at the top);
                independent of the chosen segment’s spectral centroid
                — same direct region follow
   - Envelope / duration / filter Q → continuous functions of κ/δ (and calm
                area/fill) at spawn — not binary calm|chaos presets, not RGB

   Colour chooses where in the source the grain listens. Spatial X places the
   grain in the stereo field and selects which source channel feeds the
   grain. Colour must never set loudness, allocation, or topology. Luminance
   is not volume. Playback rate stays ±1 (no Doppler).

   Two axes, two consequences: timeOrder (stability + extent) → duration and
   envelope; spatial similarity → Q. Still-and-uniform and still-and-detailed
   differ in material at matched loudness. Q is spectral purity, not loudness;
   the bandpass is unity-gain at its centre frequency.


3. Field observation (how we see regimes)

   Every CA step, with spatial and temporal averaging so regimes are stable
   enough to hear (not micro-flicker):

   - δ   local rate of change (smoothed colour/state delta)
   - s   local colour similarity (inverse of neighbour variance)
   - κ   coherence = similarity × stability (high only when BOTH hold)
   - extent via region width / height / area (spatial scale of a coherent mass)

   Group into observational regions:

   - Calm: similar colour, slow changing, with measurable area A, centre of
     mass (x̄, ȳ), mean change δ̄, mean coherence κ̄, bounding width/height,
     and COM velocity from smoothed COM motion. A solid block that sits still
     or slides cell→adjacent cell as a continuous body stays here.
   - Textured: spatially dissimilar but temporally stable — static detail is
     NOT chaos. Heard as few long overlapping grains whose identity diversity
     carries the texture. A frozen universe sounds still.
   - Oscillator groups: cells whose colour repeats with a confirmed period
     (2..8 steps) are structure, not noise. Grouped by period, re-derived
     every frame; excluded from calm/texture/chaos bags. Grains fire in
     bursts phase-locked to the visual period.
   - Flow groups: a colour that reappears nearby along a heading (similarity
     travel). Fifth area-share pool; conveyor grains on FLOW_ID_BASE tracks.
     Full borders in “Flow observation + grain pool” below.
   - Chaotic area: genuinely changing remainder — not periodic, not coherent
     correspondence. Treated as a bag of cells; chaos grains spawn δ-weighted
     so change is the event.

   δ is spatially averaged (3×3) before regime decisions so single-cell
   speckle cannot masquerade as chaos.

   Regions are re-derived each frame with smoothing. They are measurements,
   not voice owners. Do not score “interestingness” or prefer one regime.


4. Region laws → grain function (how grains behave)

   Spawn rate     ∝ region mean change δ̄
                  (faster change → more frequent fires)

   Duration       ∝ κ̄ and grows with area A
                  (large calm areas → long grains)

   Overlap        ∝ κ̄ × size
                  (calm → overlap allowed; chaos → overlap ≈ 0)

   Calm packing:  few long overlapping grains → one continuous texture
   Chaos packing: many short non-overlapping grains from random cells
                  inside the chaotic area. Chaos rate is pure change
                  (δ̄→0 → silence). CHAOS_EVENTS_MAX_HZ is a CPU rail only.

   Percussiveness is a coverage consequence, not an envelope setting. Event
   rate is share ÷ duration. Chaos reads as discrete events only at small
   coverage. For a pulsed pool the area share is spent at the pulse; silence
   between hits is not an unspent share.

   Sampling must not manufacture change. Calm/texture spawn sites are
   stratified and re-derived every frame from the measured area — not
   listening posts, never owned. Chaos and oscillators stay random.

   The surrounding relationships determine the laws. The grain spawn location
   supplies hue, X, and Y. The trigger and behaviour come from region
   observation.


5. Freeze at spawn (anti-glitch law)

   Frozen at birth (must not chase):

   - Sample / scrub window and ping-pong bounds
   - Playback direction
   - Envelope shape (attack/release fractions for that grain’s life)

   Allowed to track directly after birth (any grain with a region id):

   - Stereo pan ← region COM X + spawn offset (equal-power, no smoothing)
   - Spectral Y ← region COM Y + spawn offset (no smoothing)
   - Channel mix ← region COM X (same follow)

   Forbidden: modulating the sample scrub window during playback to chase a
   moving region or cell (causes undesirable glitches / pitch tears).

   Sonic time follows CA time via inter-grain scrub: each calm region / the
   texture pool advances a scrub offset for newly spawned grains. Living
   grains never move their window.

   How region texture follows motion: grains that belong to a coherent region
   move their pan and spectrum with that region; sample material stays locked;
   new grains also spawn from the updated mask/colour; overlap crossfades
   create continuous evolution. Chaos-bag grains have no region id and keep
   spawn pan/Y.


6. Geometry and colour laws at spawn (setup for each grain)

   Decided only at spawn:

   - Hue → sample window + ping-pong: spawn-cell hue maps to a sample range
     locked at spawn (calm wider than chaos). If the read head reaches either
     edge before the grain ends, it reverses and continues (ping-pong) until
     the grain’s defined length completes.
   - X → pan: spawn cell horizontal position maps to equal-power stereo pan.
   - Motion → direction: if the region’s COM is moving left/right, playback
     direction may be forward or reverse to match that motion.
   - Height → Y spread: tall coherent regions bias the few calm grains toward
     varied Y spawn heights (fuller harmonic spread). Thin/short regions keep
     a narrower Y spread.

   These are physics of colour, geometry, and motion — not aesthetic effects.


7. Finite grain budget (area spends capacity; activity does not buy priority)

   There is a maximum concurrent grain count (physical constraint).

   Each region’s share of that budget ∝ A / total area.
   Large stable areas that dominate the simulation receive most of the budget
   and spend it as few long overlapping grains. Chaotic areas receive share
   by their area and spend it as many short hits.

   Area share is spent, not merely allocated. Caps that prevent a pool from
   reaching its share are loudness policy in disguise.

   Activity (δ̄) shapes rate, length, and overlap — how the share is used.
   Activity must NOT increase loudness or sonic priority.


8. Volume is a finite neutral resource

   Master / total sonic energy stays roughly constant whether the simulation
   is all calm, all chaotic, or mixed.

   Neither chaotic nor stable regions may be louder or quieter as a policy.
   Their balance in the output follows their weighting in the simulation
   (area share → grain-budget share → texture character).
   What changes is grain usage and texture, not mix-desk preference.

   Amplitude normalisation across active grains enforces neutrality.
   More grains or faster firing must not equal a louder mix.

   Neutrality is measured, not assumed. Filter bandwidth is compensated;
   envelope energy is normalised after attack/release rescale. The source
   spectrum is not compensated. Primary loudness measure for equal-power
   stereo is total power √mean(L²+R²) — not mono fold-down.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Flow observation + grain pool
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Flow is an observation of similarity travel: a colour that reappears nearby
along a heading. Speed and direction are inferred as correspondence (where
did this colour last appear?). A pack’s centre of mass is a side effect and
is untrustworthy on long or wrapping structures.

Flow grains are a fifth area-share pool. Confirmed flow cells are removed
from the chaos/texture remainder before share allocation. Do not fold flow
back into chaos. Filled translating silhouettes stay calm; 2D scatter is
not flow.

Regime borders (overlay + Field % + scheduler pools):

  Calm     connected similar colour, large evenly filled mass. A solid
           block that sits still or slides cell→adjacent cell as a
           continuous body stays here (moving-bar, filled stamps / ribbons
           that translate as fixed silhouettes).
  Static   temporally still, spatially dissimilar (texture).
  Osc      confirmed periodic cells (period 2..8).
  Flow     a colour that travels by correspondence. Members (or 4×4
           colour-blocks) match a nearby same-colour site last frame
           along a shared heading. Colour stays itself (no hue tint walk).
           Dashed streams, wavefronts, dithered sliding texture, and
           denser cascades count if heading is consistent and the pack is
           not a filled stamp. Thin travelling colour may ride the edge
           of a calm mass.
           Confirm ~8 steps + travel floor ≥ ~1.5 cells. Size ~4 (keep 3).
           Field mode colour bin is ignored so motion *wake* does not
           glue into flow; a travelling rare hue is kept.
           Reject: solid high-fill slabs; high persist with filled AABB
           (sliding bodies); stamp-like density / packT; emerging
           oscillators that are sitting still; isolated 2D scatter;
           scramble-as-one-flow; stationary flicker.
           Two mono-colour streams may coexist. No “moving static” pool.
  Chaos    changing remainder — not periodic, not coherent correspondence.

Overlay (one colour per regime, not per region id): gold calm, grey static,
orange chaos, blue osc, green flow. Drawn as 1px outlines at display
resolution — no interior wash. Tracked calm / oscillator / flow regions
get L-brackets and a COM mark; chaos is edge fragments. Live grains are
ticks on the source waveform under the field, not dots on the CA.

Detection (cheap): 4×4 spatial×colour blocks → match each to the previous
same-hue block → cluster by colour + heading + lane. Not flood-fill.
Track identity may follow colour+size when COM jumps (wrapping fields).

Flow grain laws:

  - Share ∝ Σ flow.regionArea (occupied patch, same as Flow %).
  - Spawn on trailing-edge member cells (start of hop motion), never the
    COM or dilated gaps. Extra concurrent grains space along the heading.
  - Pack-to-share; duration in a mid chaos–calm band from packing density
    (area / regionArea → FLOW_ORDER_MIN..MAX), optionally capped by stream
    length / hop speed so a grain dies near the leading edge. RegionArea
    buys concurrency; packing shapes duration/envelope. Chaos stays
    area-blind (short).
  - Envelope from that timeOrder; polar material bias neutral;
    FLOW_WINDOW_T mixes sat→window toward the long end (less choppy
    than chaos). Sample scrub width is not driven by packing.
  - Pan / Y / channelMix follow via applyTracks with
    regionId = FLOW_ID_BASE + flow.id (disjoint from calm ids).
    Follow hop-velocity conveyor (velX/velY), not group COM deltas.
    When a grain reaches the leading edge, end it; spawn the next at
    the trailing edge.
  - Sample window frozen at spawn. Playback direction may freeze from
    velX (same as calm) — that is not Doppler.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Audio Engine as Constrained Renderer
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The audio engine does not decide what deserves to be heard, and does not ask
what a fixed point in space should sound like.

It asks:

  What sonic events naturally emerge from the current computational state
  and evolution of the simulation?

Its only legitimate constraints are physical and perceptual:

- Maximum grain count
- Maximum total sonic energy (held neutral)
- Maximum CPU budget
- Human perceptual limitations

Rules must be simple and mathematically explainable — not artistic judgement
about what is musical, ambient, beautiful, chaotic, or interesting.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Stability, Chaos, and Neutrality
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The engine must not impose a sonic aesthetic on the simulation.

Stable regimes should not be forced into ambient pads.
Chaotic regimes should not be forced into glitch music.

If the simulation is calm, chaotic, propagating, collapsing, or transforming,
the sonic texture should naturally reflect those behaviours because grain
rate, length, overlap, and spawn patterns emerge from coherence and change —
not because the engine chose a mood.

If the simulation is entirely chaotic and fast-changing, the audio should be
too. If it is half chaotic and half stable by area, grain-budget share and
texture should reflect that split — at neutral loudness.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source Audio
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The user may load WAV, MP3, or AIFF.

Source audio is raw material from which grains are formed. The CA’s evolution
redistributes exploration of that material through the Sonic Laws. No
traditional effects rack is required for the listening philosophy.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Visual Engine
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Utomata remains the Cellular Automata visual engine.

It provides cell positions, RGB values, neighbour relationships, temporal
evolution, global state, editable equations, and toroidal topology.

The audio renderer observes the same state. It does not drive the CA.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
User Interaction
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Users should be able to:

- Move through CA equation variations
- Restart / reset the simulation
- Load source audio
- Observe audiovisual evolution in real time

The creative instrument is the simulation.

Live pipeline
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Utomata → FrameObserver
    → FieldObserver (δ, s, κ + region extract; chaos bag; oscillator groups;
                     flow groups)
    → GrainScheduler (five area-share pools; polar HSV material; X→pan +
                      channel mix; absolute Y; freeze-at-spawn window;
                      region-bound pan/Y/channelMix follow; flow conveyor)
    → AudioEngine.sendEvents (+ track)
    → grain-processor (locked window, ping-pong, per-grain bandpass,
                       analytic envelope, equal-power pan, applyTracks)


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sacred / Negotiable / Forbidden
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SACRED
- Audio IS the CA
- Sonic Laws above (identity, observation, region laws, freeze-at-spawn,
  geometry-at-spawn, area-weighted budget, neutral loudness, flow as
  similarity travel)
- No aesthetic imposition (beauty / wash / chaos / interestingness)
- No structure/voice ownership or interestingness scoring
- No fixed listening points / persistent sonic locations as architecture
- Physically constrained rendering (grain count, energy, CPU, perception)
- Evolving sound inseparable from evolving simulation behaviour
- Observational regions measure the field; they do not own voices

NEGOTIABLE
- Exact numeric curves, thresholds, EMA times, grain budget N, hop ranges
- Grid size, CPU budgets, UI metering
- Debug visualisation of regions (optional; must not become lattice ears)

FORBIDDEN
- Fixed listening-point / lattice-pixel listening architectures
- Voice ownership / structure prioritisation / interestingness scores
- Using luminance→volume as the primary way to “hear the CA”
- Mid-grain sample-window scrub chase of moving regions
  (direct pan / Y-spectrum / channel-mix follow for region-bound grains
  is allowed; no smoothing)
- Colour → loudness, allocation, or macro schedule
- Treating the CA as a modulation source for an unrelated granular synth
- Optimising for ambient beauty, glitch excitement, or “musicality”
- Letting high activity increase loudness or steal budget beyond area share
- Doppler / velocity→grain rate (pitch offsets beyond ±1)
- Caps that prevent a pool from spending its area share
- Treating CHAOS_EVENTS_MAX_HZ as a mix knob
- A sixth “moving static” regime (calm covers translating silhouettes)
- Treating a filled translating silhouette as flow; treating 2D
  same-direction scatter as one flow; tint-glue of hues
- Folding flow grains back into chaos share; applyTracks on raw flow.id
  (collides with calm — use FLOW_ID_BASE)
- Reviving X→sample / RGB→envelope, the 48-bin spectral bank, or a 1D
  hue→centroid LUT as the sole material axis


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Success / Failure
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Success: the listener feels they are hearing the simulation’s behaviour evolve —
calm, chaos, flow, propagation, collapse, transformation — as continuous
changing sonic texture. Large coherent areas form long overlapping textures
that evolve as successive grains follow regional motion; chaotic areas spray
short grains; travelling colour rides a conveyor; overall loudness stays
neutral; balance matches area weighting.

Failure: constant repeating texture; curated voices; fixed posts; aesthetic
mood engines; mid-grain glitchy scrub chasing; chaos winning the mix by firing
more; or “granular synth controlled by CA.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Final Creative Goal
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Create an audiovisual instrument that behaves as a miniature computational
universe.

The beauty of the visuals emerges from simple local relationships between cells.
The beauty of the sound emerges from those same relationships heard as
computational flow through time — grains as events of that flow — rendered as
faithfully as physics and perception allow under the Sonic Laws.
