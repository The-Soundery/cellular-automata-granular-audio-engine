### 4d deviation — the guard as written does not fire on `moving-bar`

The plan's guard is `region.width >= obs.width`. Measured on the real observer,
the `moving-bar` background is **w = 117, h = 128**, so the x-guard never fires,
and its `comX` advances **+1.00 cells/step** — the background's centre of mass is
dragged by the moving hole, not by its own mass. Result: pan spread **2.00**,
failing gate (i) outright. This contradicts the plan's own prototype figure of
0.20 cells/step for that background, so the literal guard cannot be what was
prototyped.

Generalised the guard to the statistic §4d is actually describing — the circular
mean resultant length R of the members about each axis. R → 0 is exactly "this
axis has no meaningful circular mean"; the threshold is the textbook circular
variance midpoint, R < 0.5, not a fitted number. The plan's literal
`width >= obs.width` test is retained alongside it. Measured R:

### Spectral flux: the Phase 1 estimator was measuring its own variance

A single 4096-point periodogram of a noise-driven signal has Rayleigh-distributed
bins, so the L2 distance between two independent unit-energy spectra is
`sqrt(2 − π/2) = 0.6551` regardless of the signal. Every scenario measured
0.6191–0.6567 — i.e. the noise floor. Validated against synthetic ground truth:

| signal | true flux | old estimator | new estimator |
|---|---|---|---|
| stationary filtered noise | 0 | 0.6539 | 0.0982 |
| stationary white noise | 0 | 0.6557 | 0.0654 |
| slow spectral sweep | mid | 0.6641 | 0.1506 |
| 100 ms timbre alternation | high | 1.1785 | 1.0525 |

Replaced with the same measure computed on Welch-averaged (512-pt Hann, hop 64),
16-mel-band spectra per 100 ms block. Block length, normalisation and the L2
difference are unchanged.
